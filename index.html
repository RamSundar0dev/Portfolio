About

I'm a senior software developer based in Tamil Nadu, India, currently leading a team of up to six developers at Onexfort. I own delivery end to end — from architecture and code review through to deployment — across web and mobile products.

A lot of my work happens with clients, not just code. Early in my career I was the person clients spoke to before signing, and I converted 8 of every 10 pre-sales conversations into projects. More recently I've gathered requirements on-site for BOS, translating what a business actually needs into a working platform.

My core stack is React, Next.js, Laravel and Node.js, and I build the modern pieces around them: LLM-powered features and RAG, Stripe payments, real-time messaging with WebSockets, and production infrastructure on AWS.
