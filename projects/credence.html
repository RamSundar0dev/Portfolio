Overview

Credence Community Capital is internal software for managing and maintaining customer loans. I designed and implemented the features that let customers view their loan details and pay their monthly dues seamlessly.

The focus was a user-friendly interface backed by a robust, secure system for reliable transactions and efficient loan management.

What I did

  • Designed and implemented customer-facing loan viewing and payment features.
  • Built the front end in Angular and the backend with CodeIgniter and MySQL.
  • Prioritised secure transactions and dependable loan management throughout.
