Overview

Planq brings AI-powered retrieval to its users through a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline running over a vector database, so responses are grounded in the right context rather than guessed.

I worked on the retrieval architecture and the application around it, connecting the RAG pipeline to a clean Next.js front end backed by Laravel and PostgreSQL.

What I did

  • Built an AI retrieval pipeline using RAG over a vector database for context-aware responses.
  • Developed the application with Laravel, Next.js and PostgreSQL.
  • Connected the AI layer to the product experience end to end.
