BOS — Business Operating System

An AI-native platform that unifies CRM, ERP, inventory, manufacturing and HR into a single operational foundation for a whole business.

Visit itsbos.com ↗

Overview

BOS — short for Business Operating System — is an AI-native platform that replaces the usual stack of disconnected tools (CRM, ERP, inventory, manufacturing, HR, spreadsheets) with one operational foundation for the entire business.

This was a project where I worked closely with the client from the very start: I visited their locations in person to understand how the business actually runs, then helped translate those findings into a working application.

What I did

  • Gathered business requirements first-hand through on-site visits to client locations.
  • Built the platform with Next.js, Python and PostgreSQL.
  • Integrated LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic) to power the product's AI-native features.
  • Helped turn a broad, cross-department problem into a focused, buildable roadmap.
